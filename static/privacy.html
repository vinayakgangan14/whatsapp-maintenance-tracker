Privacy Policy for Maintenance Tracker

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Application: Maintenance Tracker & Industrial Plant Management Portal ("the App")

1. Overview

Maintenance Tracker is an industrial maintenance management application designed for manufacturing plants, facilities, and engineering teams to track machine breakdowns, scheduled preventive maintenance (PM), welding logs, and downtime metrics.

2. Information We Collect

To provide industrial logging and equipment tracking services, the App may collect:

  • User Account Information: Username, email address, company name, and designated role (Administrator, Manager, or Operator) for authentication and access control.
  • Operational & Maintenance Data: Machine IDs, department names, breakdown descriptions, repair logs, scheduled maintenance dates, and duration timestamps.
  • Optional Media / Camera Uploads: When operators or technicians upload photos of damaged machinery or completed repairs. Media is strictly used within your organization's maintenance records.

3. How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and authorize personnel to their respective plant workspaces.
  • Track machine downtime, Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), and Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF).
  • Notify team members of ticket assignments, approvals, and resolutions via web portal and WhatsApp notifications.
  • Sync records securely to your organization's designated cloud storage (Supabase / Google Sheets).

4. Data Storage & Security

All network communication is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS). Data is securely stored in cloud databases with encrypted connections. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or operational data with any third-party advertisers.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users or company administrators can request the review, update, or deletion of their account information and company records at any time directly through the administrator dashboard or by contacting the system administrator.
